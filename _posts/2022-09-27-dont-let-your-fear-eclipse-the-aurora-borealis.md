![Northern Lights](/assets/blog/Northern+Lights.webp)

I was on a plane from London to New York. I had a window seat. I was looking out of the window and I saw the most beautiful Aurora Borealis. It was green and purple and it was dancing across the sky. I was so excited. I wanted to take a picture. I reached for my phone. But I was scared. I was scared that if I took my phone out, I would drop it. I was scared that if I took a picture, it wouldn't come out right. I was scared that I would miss the moment. So I didn't take a picture. I just watched. And then it was gone.

I was so disappointed. I had missed the opportunity to capture that moment. I had let my fear get in the way. I had let my fear eclipse the Aurora Borealis.

How many times have you let your fear get in the way of something you wanted to do? How many times have you let your fear eclipse the Aurora Borealis?

Fear is a powerful emotion. It can stop us from doing things we want to do. It can stop us from taking risks. It can stop us from living our lives to the fullest.

But fear is also a liar. It tells us that we can't do something. It tells us that we will fail. It tells us that we will be embarrassed. But the truth is, we can do it. We can succeed. We can be proud of ourselves.

So the next time you are scared to do something, remember the Aurora Borealis. Remember that if you let your fear get in the way, you will miss out on something beautiful. So take a deep breath, and go for it.
