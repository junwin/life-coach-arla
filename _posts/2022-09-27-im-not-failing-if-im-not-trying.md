date: 2022-09-27

Arla realized she'd been avoiding restarting her weight management coaching group because she'd regained weight and felt like an imposter. Then it hit her: she wasn't actually *trying* to lose weight right now, so how could she be failing at it?

A nice reframe — failure requires effort. If you're not trying, you're just... not trying.
