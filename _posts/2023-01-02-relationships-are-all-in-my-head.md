This is the last day of the year in which I said goodbye to my last and special aunt and my last and special uncle. As the calendar flips to 2023 tonight, my thoughts are trying to tell me that I am leaving them behind in 2022.

When my mom died in 2019, and when my brother died in 2021, I had the same thought on the last day of each of those years, too.

But what if I'm not leaving them behind at all? What if I worked on believing what I've learned from life coaching myself and others: **My relationships are all in my head.**

Even when these important people were alive, my relationships with my aunt, my uncle, my brother and my mom were in my head. **Sounds crazy, right? Except it isn't.**

Each thought I believe about someone creates a feeling. The feeling I create is what influences my relationships with them.

**For example:** When my mom was alive, I used to think, "my mom really knows how to love." That made me smile so hard my face hurt. I felt lucky. And when I felt lucky:
- I remembered all the ways she had of making me feel loved
- I was inspired
- I tried to emulate her
- I told my people more often that I love them

Even though she is now physically gone, I still think, "my mom really knew how to love." Thinking that thought still makes me smile until my face hurts. I still feel so lucky, and I still behave in the same ways. I create an amazing, close relationship with her. **Exactly the same.**

So this evening, I am reminding myself that I am not leaving Aunt Mimi and Uncle Winky behind in 2022. All I have to do is to think the same things about them that I've always thought. And that will keep them alive in my head — and in my heart — forever.

**Wishing you a happy, healthy and prosperous New Year!**
