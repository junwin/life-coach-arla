*Feb 14, 2023*

**Be Your Valentine!**

Okay, so I'm in the UK this week, where it is currently 8:57 pm on February 13, 2023. That means only 3 hours until the clock kicks over into February 14, 2023.

**AHHHH!**

February 14 is just another day on the calendar. And it happens to be the date that has been designated as Valentine's Day. It doesn't have to be an annual day from hell. You don't have to hate it. You don't have to feel bad about it. And you for sure don't have to think there is something wrong with you if you don't have a date for the day.

**IT'S JUST A DATE ON THE CALENDAR. IT IS NEUTRAL. IT HAS NO POSITIVE OR NEGATIVE MEANING AT ALL.**

All of the drama around the day is created by you; by your thoughts. They are just sentences in your brain ABOUT Valentine's Day — no matter how much you might want to believe they are true and factual.

Your thoughts about Valentine's Day create your feelings about Valentine's Day.

For example, "I'm a loser on Valentine's Day because no one loves me" — those thoughts may make you feel **UNWORTHY AND UNLOVABLE.**

**HEY! THIS IS ALL GOOD NEWS!** These unhelpful thoughts are **COMPLETELY OPTIONAL.**

When I think (and believe): "Valentine's Day is just another day, and my not having a boyfriend or not being married or not receiving flowers doesn't say anything about me" — **I FEEL EMPOWERED AND I FEEL GOOD ABOUT MYSELF!**

So tomorrow, why not try something different? Try on some new thoughts in advance. Find the thought that fits; that helps you feel a little better; and practice it a few times throughout the day.
