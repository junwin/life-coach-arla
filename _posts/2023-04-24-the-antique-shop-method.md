*Apr 24, 2023*

As usual, I was walking the dog this morning, minding my own business when POW! I had another epiphany about thoughts (my favorite subject).

My clients will often ask me how the heck they can identify just one thought at a time. Our brains seem to want to offer us so many thoughts so quickly that we may feel overwhelmed.

It struck me that this is a little like how I used to feel when going into antique shops. I'd walk in, look around and think "what a bunch of dusty old junk."

One rainy day while weekending with friends in Lakeside, Michigan, I accompanied friends to multiple antique stores. This time, expecting to be bored, I actually saw an item that interested me — a darling little china vase with tiny roses on it, that I now own.

I stopped and picked up the cutie vase. And found I wanted to keep going down the aisle to see what other hidden gems there might be. I looked at one item at a time. And once I focused on one object at a time, I saw LOTS of other interesting things!

**The point:** Your first few looks inside your brain may be like my first look around the antique shops. There may be overwhelm when you try to discover the thoughts being offered. But once you pull out one thought at a time to examine it, the overwhelm goes away and you get to decide if you want to "buy it" or try on a different one.

Because: Your thoughts cause your feelings. Your feelings cause your actions. And your actions are responsible for all of the results you've created in your life.
