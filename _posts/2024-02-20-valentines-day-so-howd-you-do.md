When last we met, 'twas the day before Valentine's Day. So, I just gotta ask:

**How'd it go?** 😍😜😞💪

If you're reading this, you obviously made it through that craziest of days, that Hallmark and Fannie May day that's right up there with New Year's Eve and your birthday. You know, the days of the year on top of which we heap so much expectation and drama.

So, how'd you do?

- What did you make the day mean about you? About other people?
- Were you able to pull up your emergency thought as needed throughout the day?
- And what about the day after?
- Chocolate hangover? Crying-jag headache?
- What thoughts did you try on to help you feel the way you wanted to feel the next day?

Regardless of whether you recognized Valentine's Day or decided to treat it like any other day on the calendar, I hope you heaped a whole lot of love upon yourself.
